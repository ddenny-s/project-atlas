## Disposition Vocabulary

- **Keep** when current behavior is evidenced, necessary, and proportionate.
- **Rewrite** when the owning mechanism cannot safely meet the target contract.
- **Delete** when reachability and replacement evidence show the contour is obsolete.
- **Merge** when overlapping contours can share one clear owner without hiding differences.
