## Security

Map trust, authentication, authorization, secret, input, and external-provider boundaries without copying sensitive values.

## Reliability and Recovery

Map timeout, retry, idempotency, ordering, cancellation, rollback, restore, and partial-state behavior.

## Observability and Operations

Map logs, metrics, traces, alerts, runbooks, backup, restore, and operator authority.

## Coverage Limits

Record exact denominators and exclusions. Do not equate green tests with production proof.
